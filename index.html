ThreeTasks

Shake to decide

Can't decide what to do first? Keep a Morning, Daytime and Evening list, then shake your phone. ThreeTasks picks one task from each, with fireworks to get you going.

  • ☀️One task for each part of the day
  • 🔁One-off or recurring tasks
  • 🔔Optional reminders and a Home Screen widget
  • 🔒No account, no ads, no tracking
